# Adidas Sales Performance Analysis and Dashboard
Analysis of sales data with retailers, product, regional, and temporal attributes for analyzing revenue, profit, pricing, and seasonal trends.

## 🚀 Project Overview
This repository contains sales dataset and a dashboard aimed at analyzing overall sales performance across multiple regions, products, retailers, and sales channels. The project focuses on delivering actionable insights related to revenue trends, profitability, pricing strategies, and seasonal patterns to support data-driven business decision-making.

## 🗂️ Dataset Description
The dataset includes transactional sales records with the following key columns:
- **Retailer**: Name of retail outlets
- **Retailer ID**: Unique identifier for each retailer
- **Invoice Date**: Date of transaction
- **Region**: Geographic sales region
- **State**: State within the region
- **City**: City of sale
- **Product**: Sold product name
- **Price per Unit**: Unit sale price
- **Units Sold**: Quantity sold
- **Total Sales**: Revenue generated (Price per Unit × Units Sold)
- **Operating Profit**: Profit earned from sales
- **Operating Margin**: Profit margin percentage
- **Sales Method**: Sales channel (i.e. Online, In-store, Outlet)

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Explore overall sales performance across regions.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
- Compare revenue and units sold by retailer and sales channel.
- Analyze profit margins and pricing trends.
- Discover seasonal and regional sales patterns.
- Provide recommendations to improve sales and profitability.

## 🛠️ Methodology

### Data Preparation
- Data validation performed to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Computation of the Total Sales Column (Price per unit x units sold) to reflect accuracy
- Calculation of the Operating Profit (Total Sales/Operating Margin) to ensure accuracy. 

### Exploratory Data Analysis
- Utilization of Excel PivotTables to summarize sales, profit, units, and margins by multiple dimensions (Region, Product, Retailer, Sales Method, Time).
- Interactive slicers implemented for filtering and drill-down capability.
- Visual identification of trends and seasonality.

### Development
- KPI cards designed to highlight key metrics including Total Transactions, Total Sales, Total Operating Profit, Average Operating Margin, and Total Units Sold.
- Six core KPI charts developed:
  - Regional Sales Performance
  - Product by Sales Analysis
  - Retailer and Sales Method Comparison
  - Sales Method By Total Units Sold 
  - Monthly Sales and Profit Trend 
  - Top 5 States by Total Sales Contribution 
- Use of color coding and clear labeling for readability and quick insights.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Concentrate marketing and inventory on top regions (West & North East) and high-performing products (Men's Street Footwear, Women's Apparel, Men's Athletic Footwear) to maximise ROI.
- Investigate products like Women's Athletic Footwear and Men's Apparel. Consider promotions, bundling strategies, or product repositioning.
- Strengthen physical store offerings, improve inventory management, and use stores to drive online engagement. Address online channel weaknesses as in-store purchases dominate.
- Reinforce alliances with key retailers such as West Gear and Foot Locker, replicating successful channel strategies with other partners.
- Align inventory and marketing calendars with observed seasonality to effectively capture peak demand and mitigate low periods.
- Evaluate pricing and reduce unnecessary discounts on high-selling but lower-profit products to enhance overall profitability.
